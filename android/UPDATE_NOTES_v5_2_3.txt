FretSense v5.2.3

Fixed capo/transposed play display:
- Timeline now shows the chord shape the musician should physically play.
- PLAY NOW now shows the playable capo shape.
- Chord diagram now follows the playable capo shape.
- A small "sounds X" label preserves the actual sounding chord.
- Timeline badge changes to CAPO N when capo mode is active.
- Common chords and Practice mode now follow capo shapes too.
- Key transpose still applies first, then capo shapes are calculated from that active key.

Example: Original A#m + capo 1 => PLAY NOW Am, diagram Am, small label sounds A#m.
