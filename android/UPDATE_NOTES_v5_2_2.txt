FretSense Multi-Instrument Android v5.2.2

FIX: Active chord diagram now follows the active sounding chord.

Behavior:
- Transpose = 0: the diagram shows the original detected chord.
- Transpose changed: the diagram changes to the transposed chord everywhere.
- Capo does NOT replace the main diagram with the easy capo shape.
- The capo-friendly shape remains shown separately below the diagram.

Example:
Active chord: A#m
Capo: 1
Main diagram: A#m (x 1 3 3 2 1)
Capo-friendly instruction: play Am shape with capo 1

Added movable guitar diagrams for sharp/flat major and minor chords and common 7th qualities.
